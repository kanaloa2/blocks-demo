The problem

Two bad options. Advisors deserve better.

Advisors today face an uncomfortable tradeoff: spend hours doing it yourself, or outsource and give up control — and a big chunk of your margin.

💸

Outsourcing is expensive

A typical UMA or TAMP charges 20–40bps on AUM. On a $200M book, that's $400,000–$800,000 a year — for a model you didn't build and can't easily customize.

⏱

Doing it yourself takes too long

Building portfolios in-house means hours of manual screening, disconnected spreadsheets, and the same grunt work repeated for every new client or rebalance.

🔔

Either way, drift goes unnoticed

Client portfolios silently drift from targets. By the time it's caught — whether you're DIY or outsourced — rebalancing costs are higher and clients are already exposed.

Blocks is the third option.
If you outsource today, Blocks can replace your TAMP or UMA — and put that 20–40bps back in your pocket while giving you full control over what goes in each portfolio. If you already build in-house, Blocks replaces the spreadsheets and manual screening with AI that does the grunt work in seconds — so you spend your time on judgment, not research.

How it works

From idea to portfolio in three steps.

Blocks turns a natural language description into a fully constructed, monitored portfolio — with the advisor in control at every step.

01
Describe your block
Type what you're looking for in plain English — "defensive dividend payers with low volatility" or "high-growth tech, profitable only." No query language, no filters to hunt through.
✦ Natural language screening
02
Review AI candidates
Blocks returns a ranked list of securities scored on your criteria — with AI-generated rationale for each. Accept, reject, or adjust. You're always the final decision-maker.
✦ AI-scored + explained
03
Assemble portfolios
Combine blocks into client portfolios. The AI watches for drift, flags correlation issues, and suggests rebalancing trades — while you stay focused on client relationships.
✦ Continuous monitoring
Features

Everything an advisor needs. Nothing they don't.

Built specifically for the portfolio construction workflow — not adapted from generic financial software.

Block Library
Build a reusable library of curated investment blocks. Use them across any client portfolio, update once and propagate everywhere.
AI Security Screening
Describe what you want in plain English. The AI screens thousands of securities in seconds and returns a ranked, annotated shortlist.
Drift Monitoring
Automatic alerts when portfolios drift from their target allocation. Catch it early, before rebalancing costs compound.
